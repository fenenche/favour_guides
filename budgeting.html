Budgeting

Simple ways to plan and manage your money.

Tracking Your Spending

Write down or use an app to log what you spend money on each week. Seeing it clearly makes it much easier to spot where your money actually goes.

The 50/30/20 Rule

A simple guide: spend about 50% of your money on needs, 30% on wants, and save or pay off debt with the remaining 20%.

Setting Savings Goals

Give your savings a purpose, like a new phone or emergency fund. A specific goal makes it easier to stay motivated and avoid impulse spending.
