What it takes to start and run a small business.

Finding a Business Idea

Good business ideas often solve a problem people already have. Think about challenges you or people around you face regularly.

Basic Business Planning

A simple plan should cover what you're selling, who your customers are, and how you'll reach them. It doesn't need to be complicated to start.

Managing Money Wisely

Keep your personal and business money separate from the start, and always track what's coming in and going out.
