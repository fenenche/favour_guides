Nutrition Basics

Simple, practical guidance on eating well and building healthy habits.

Balanced Meals

A balanced meal generally includes a mix of carbohydrates, protein, and vegetables. Try to fill half your plate with vegetables or fruit when you can.

Staying Hydrated

Drinking enough water each day helps your body function properly, supports concentration, and prevents fatigue.

Healthy Snacking

Instead of sugary or heavily processed snacks, try fruit, nuts, or yogurt to keep your energy steady.
