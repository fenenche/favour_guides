Nutrition Basics

Simple, practical guidance on eating well and building healthy habits.

Balanced Meals

A balanced meal generally includes a mix of carbohydrates (like rice, bread, or yam), protein (like beans, fish, or eggs), and vegetables. Try to fill half your plate with vegetables or fruit when you can.

Staying Hydrated

Drinking enough water each day helps your body function properly, supports concentration, and prevents fatigue. A common guide is to aim for about 8 cups of water a day, adjusting for activity level and climate.

Healthy Snacking

Instead of reaching for sugary or heavily processed snacks, try fruit, nuts, or yogurt. These keep your energy steady instead of causing quick spikes and crashes.
