Useful Apps & Tools

Simple tools that can make learning and daily life easier.

Note-Taking Apps

Apps like Google Keep or Notion let you quickly jot down ideas and organize notes by subject.

Productivity Tools

Google Calendar helps track deadlines, while Google Docs lets you write and share documents easily.

Learning Resources

Free platforms like Khan Academy and YouTube offer lessons on almost any subject.
