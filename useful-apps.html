Useful Apps & Tools

Simple tools that can make learning and daily life easier.

Note-Taking Apps

Apps like Google Keep or Notion let you quickly jot down ideas, make checklists, and organize notes by subject — much easier to search than paper notes.

Productivity Tools

Tools like Google Calendar help you keep track of deadlines and plans, while apps like Google Docs let you write and share documents without needing to email files back and forth.

Learning Resources

Free platforms like Khan Academy and YouTube offer lessons on almost any subject. They're great for revisiting topics you find difficult in class.
