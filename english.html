English Language

A guide covering grammar, comprehension, and essay writing for junior secondary to high school students.

Grammar Basics

A sentence needs at least a subject and a verb to be complete. Watch for subject-verb agreement and consistent verb tense.

Reading Comprehension

Read the passage fully first, then the questions, then find the exact part of the passage that answers each one.

Essay Writing

A good essay has an introduction stating your main point, body paragraphs with supporting details, and a conclusion summing up your argument.
