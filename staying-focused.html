Staying Focused

Ways to avoid distractions and concentrate while studying.

Removing Distractions

Put your phone in another room or block distracting notifications while you study.

The Pomodoro Technique

Study in focused 25-minute sessions, followed by a 5-minute break. Take a longer break after four sessions.

Setting Clear Goals

Decide exactly what you want to accomplish before you start, like 'finish 10 math problems.'
