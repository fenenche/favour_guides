Time Management

How to plan your study time and avoid last-minute cramming.

Making a Study Schedule

Break study time into 30-45 minute blocks per subject, with short breaks in between.

Prioritizing Tasks

Start with subjects due soonest or that you find hardest, while your mind is fresh.

Avoiding Procrastination

Break big tasks into smaller steps. Starting with just 5-10 minutes often makes it easier to keep going.
