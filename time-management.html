Time Management

How to plan your study time and avoid last-minute cramming.

Making a Study Schedule

Break your study time into manageable blocks, like 30-45 minutes per subject, with short breaks in between. Write it down or use a calendar app so you can see your plan clearly.

Prioritizing Tasks

Start with the subjects or assignments that are due soonest or that you find hardest. Tackling difficult tasks when your mind is fresh makes them easier to get through.

Avoiding Procrastination

Break big tasks into smaller steps so they feel less overwhelming. Starting with just 5-10 minutes of work often makes it easier to keep going once you've begun.
