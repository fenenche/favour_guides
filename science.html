Science

Core concepts in biology, chemistry, and physics for junior secondary to high school students.

Biology Basics

Living things are made of cells. Photosynthesis is how plants make food using sunlight, water, and carbon dioxide.

Chemistry Basics

Everything is made of atoms, which combine to form molecules. The periodic table organizes all known elements.

Physics Basics

Newton's Laws of Motion describe how objects move and respond to forces. Energy changes form but is never created or destroyed.
