Saving & Investing

The basics of growing your money over time.

Why Saving Matters

Saving gives you a cushion for emergencies and helps you afford bigger goals later, like further education or starting a business.

Understanding Interest

When you save money in a bank account, it can earn interest over time, meaning your money grows just by sitting there.

What Investing Means

Investing means putting money into something (like stocks or a business) with the goal of it growing in value over time. It carries more risk than saving, but also more potential reward.
