Internet Safety

How to stay safe online and protect your personal information.

Protecting Your Information

Avoid sharing personal details like your address or passwords with people you don't know online.

Strong Passwords

Use different passwords for different accounts, mixing letters, numbers, and symbols. Avoid obvious things like your name.

Spotting Scams

Be wary of messages asking you to click suspicious links or send money urgently. If it feels off, it probably is.
