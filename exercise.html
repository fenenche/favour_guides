Exercise & Fitness

Easy, practical ways to stay active, even with a busy schedule.

Why Exercise Matters

Regular physical activity strengthens your heart, improves mood, and boosts energy levels.

Simple Ways to Stay Active

Take the stairs, walk or cycle for short trips, or do a 15-minute home workout.

Building a Routine

Start with 2-3 days a week of light activity and build up gradually. Consistency matters more than intensity.
