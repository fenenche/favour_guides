Exercise & Fitness

Easy, practical ways to stay active, even with a busy schedule.

Why Exercise Matters

Regular physical activity strengthens your heart, improves mood, and boosts energy levels. It doesn't have to mean intense workouts — even brisk walking counts as good exercise.

Simple Ways to Stay Active

Take the stairs instead of the elevator, walk or cycle for short trips, or do a 15-minute home workout. Small, consistent habits add up more than occasional intense sessions.

Building a Routine

Start with just 2-3 days a week of light activity, and gradually increase as it becomes a habit. Consistency matters more than intensity, especially when you're just starting out.
