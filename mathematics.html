Mathematics

Step-by-step explanations of core math topics for junior secondary to high school students.

Algebra Basics

Algebra uses letters (like x or y) to represent unknown numbers. An equation like x + 5 = 12 means you need to find the value of x that makes the statement true. Here, x = 7.

The key rule when solving equations: whatever you do to one side, you must do to the other. This keeps the equation balanced while you isolate the unknown.

Geometry Basics

Geometry is the study of shapes, sizes, and angles. Key shapes to know include triangles, squares, rectangles, and circles, each with their own formulas for area and perimeter.

For example, the area of a rectangle is length × width, while the area of a triangle is ½ × base × height. Knowing these formulas is essential for most geometry problems.

Working with Fractions

A fraction represents a part of a whole, written as one number over another (like ¾). The top number is the numerator, the bottom is the denominator.

To add or subtract fractions, they need the same denominator first. To multiply fractions, multiply the numerators together and the denominators together.
